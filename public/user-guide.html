Executive User Manual • 2026 Edition

Sojitz Vietnam Market Intelligence Platform

A concise, intuitive operational handbook designed for C-Suite executives, Business Development Directors, and Investment Analysts to leverage real-time market intelligence and translate strategic news into actionable commercial initiatives.

Target Audience: All Sojitz Vietnam Professionals & Tokyo Liaisons
Revision: October 2026
Architecture: Next.js + AI Triage Engine + PostgreSQL
01

System Overview

The Sojitz Vietnam Market Intelligence Platform is not a generic news aggregator. It is an AI-powered Triage and Decision-Support Engine that continuously monitors authoritative Vietnamese and Japanese publications, automatically filters noise, evaluates strategic impact, and synthesizes Suggested BD Actions tailored specifically to Sojitz's 5 core commercial divisions.

Zero Information Noise

Thousands of daily articles are filtered through stringent relevance criteria, isolating only macroeconomic policy shifts, tier-1 M&A transactions, and peer sogo shosha movements that genuinely matter.

Multi-Tier Verification

Every intelligence story is stamped with a verification badge (Verified / Partially Verified / Single Source) and backed by verified, active links to primary government and financial publishers.

Actionable BD Roadmaps

Each dossier articulates the exact responsible Sojitz division, strategic counterpart, and concrete next step required to convert intelligence into business value.

5 Strategic Business Divisions Tracked by the Platform
1. Energy & Natural Resources: Offshore wind, LNG import terminals, rooftop solar PPA, decarbonization mechanisms.
2. Infrastructure & Industrial Parks: Industrial zones (Long Duc IP), cold storage logistics, smart city infrastructure, ports.
3. Chemicals & Plastics: Commodity chemicals, recycled resin/plastics circularity, high-grade fertilizer blending.
4. Food & Retail Consumer: Modern retail distribution, food processing, agri-tech, cold chain distribution.
5. Automotive & Industrial Machinery: Vehicle assembly, commercial EV conversion, heavy machinery supply chains.
02

Daily Executive Workflow

1

Start Your Morning on the "Today" Screen (/today)

Access the Today feed at 08:30 AM. Fresh intelligence from the past 24 hours is automatically triaged and organized into three intuitive freshness buckets: Last 24 Hours, Past 72 Hours, and Older Intelligence.

2

Filter by Division & Priority Threshold

Use the division filter buttons at the top of the feed (e.g. click Energy or Infrastructure). Prioritize stories bearing a Relevance Score ≥ 8.0/10 — these indicate high commercial urgency.

3

Open the Story Dossier for Strategic Breakdown

Click on any story card to open its full dossier: explore Why It Matters To Sojitz Vietnam, positive/negative implications, regulatory context, and concrete BD next steps.

4

Submit Feedback via "Useful / Not Useful"

Click Useful or Not Useful at the bottom of the dossier. The adaptive ranking engine instantly recalibrates visibility for your team and trains the underlying model on your strategic preferences.

03

Decoding Scores & Verification Badges

Each intelligence item is evaluated across two primary quantitative dimensions: Relevance Score (impact to Sojitz Vietnam) and Confidence Score & Verification Status (editorial certainty).

Relevance Score Priority Level Strategic Meaning for Sojitz Vietnam Executive Recommendation
9.0 – 10.0 / 10 Critical / Direct Directly impacts existing Sojitz assets (e.g., Long Duc IP, Huong Sen packaging, EV joint ventures), major regulatory decrees (e.g., DPPA decree, land laws), or tier-1 peer moves by Sumitomo / Mitsubishi. Immediate C-Suite Action: Delegate within 24–48 hours to division general manager.
7.0 – 8.9 / 10 High Priority High commercial interest: M&A bidding rounds, industrial park land acquisitions, greenfield LNG or wind projects where Sojitz can bid as consortium partner or supply chain provider. BD Pipeline Addition: Incorporate into weekly division pipeline review.
4.0 – 6.9 / 10 Moderate / Sector Watch Broad industry trends, raw material price shifts, or early-stage feasibility studies without immediate procurement requests. Market Background: Skim for market context; no emergency intervention required.
1.0 – 3.9 / 10 Low / Background General consumer headlines, small peripheral transactions outside Sojitz core supply chain competencies. Deprioritized: Filtered to the bottom or omitted from executive summaries.

Source Verification Badges

The platform enforces temporal gating and multi-source cross-referencing so executives never act on unverified rumors:

VERIFIED (80% – 95%)

Multi-Source Corroborated

Confirmed across two or more independent Tier-1 sources (e.g. Ministry of Industry and Trade official gazette + VnEconomy). Timelines, milestones, and financial numbers carry maximum certainty.

PARTIALLY VERIFIED (65% – 75%)

Single Official Source

Originates from an authoritative primary source (e.g., Bao Chinh Phu / Government Portal), but secondary independent media corroboration has not yet emerged.

SINGLE SOURCE (55% – 65%)

Preliminary Lone Report

Reported by a single outlet. Flagged with a caution banner to instruct analysts to verify independently before committing capital or executive resources.

Human-in-the-Loop Feedback & AI Learning

The platform incorporates an Adaptive Ranking Engine. Reader feedback actively refines intelligence ranking both in real time and across future curation cycles:

Your Interaction Immediate Ranking Impact Long-Term AI Model Adaptation
+0.4 points added to effectiveScore.
The story elevates toward the top of company-wide feeds. 		The model associates positive weight to the underlying keywords, counterpart, and sector, boosting similar upcoming stories.
-0.6 points deducted from effectiveScore.
The story is immediately pushed to bottom-tier visibility. 		The model learns to dampen this specific theme, preventing similar low-utility items from cluttering C-Suite briefings.
Dynamic Effective Score Formula
Effective Score = Relevance Score + (0.4 × Useful) - (0.6 × Not Useful)
Because the negative penalty (-0.6) carries greater mathematical weight than the positive boost (+0.4), irrelevant items are pruned swiftly, preserving executive focus.

08

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What is the weekly briefing qualification timeframe?

Answer: The weekly reporting window opens at Monday 00:00:00 and closes Sunday 23:59:59 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh). Only events published within this strict window are eligible for that calendar week's briefing.

Q2: Why does an August project appear in an October weekly briefing?

Answer: The platform distinguishes between an initial historical groundbreaking (e.g. Sumitomo Smart City in August) and subsequent concrete regulatory or commercial milestones (e.g. Phase 1 master plan approval in October). New milestones represent actionable business catalysts for that current week.

Q3: Can I share direct links with colleagues?

Answer: Yes. Every story dossier and weekly report maintains a permanent direct URL (e.g. /story/[id] or /weekly/W41). You can copy and share them instantly via Microsoft Teams or corporate email.

Luồng Sử Dụng Hàng Ngày Cho Lãnh Đạo & BD

1

Bắt Đầu Ngày Mới Tại Trang "Today" (/today)

Mở trang Today vào 8:30 sáng. Hệ thống đã tự động tổng hợp các tin thị trường mới nhất trong 24 giờ qua được xếp theo 3 giỏ thời gian rõ ràng: Last 24 Hours, Past 72 Hours và Older Intelligence.

2

Lọc Nhanh Theo Khối Ngành Hoặc Điểm Quan Trọng

Sử dụng thanh bộ lọc ở đầu trang để chọn khối ngành phụ trách. Chú ý các tin có điểm Relevance Score ≥ 8/10 - đây là những tin cấp bách.

3

Click Vào Thẻ Tin Để Mở "Dossier" (Hồ Sơ Chi Tiết)

Nhấp vào bất kỳ tin nào để xem phân tích chuyên sâu: Why It Matters To Sojitz Vietnam, tác động rủi ro/cơ hội và bản đồ hành động BD cụ thể.

4

Bấm Useful / Not Useful Để Hệ Thống Học Sở Thích Của Bạn

Nếu tin hữu ích và chuẩn xác với công việc, bấm Useful. Nếu không liên quan, bấm Not Useful. AI sẽ tự động cá nhân hóa độ ưu tiên của bạn cho các bản tin sau.
